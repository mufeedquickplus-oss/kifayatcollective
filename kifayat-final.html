The Collective
About Kifayat
A living community of calligraphy artists and enthusiasts — across scripts, across borders, beyond language.
The Third Entity
Calligraphy is neither art nor literature. It is the third entity. For too long, calligraphy has been confined — tied to language, religion, a single culture's claim over the stroke. Kifayat exists to dissolve those borders. The qalam belongs to no one people and every people.
The Artist Deserves More
Skilled hands go unseen every day — not for lack of talent, but lack of a platform built for them. Kifayat is that platform. We celebrate the maker as much as the made. Through profiles, market access, workshops, and community — we build the ecosystem around the artist.
Community is Participation
Kifayat is not a gallery you visit and leave. It is a collective you belong to. Workshops, field visits, podcasts, discussions, campus chapters — a living network where everyone grows together.
Art Must Circulate
From canvas and décor to interiors, typography, merchandise, and corporate identity — calligraphy is not a niche. It is a medium that can inhabit every surface, every space that carries meaning.
