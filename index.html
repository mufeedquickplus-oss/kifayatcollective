The Collective
1 / 4
01

Calligraphy is neither art nor literature. It is the third entity.

For too long, calligraphy has been confined — tied to language, religion, a single culture's claim over the stroke. Kifayat exists to dissolve those borders. The qalam belongs to no one people and every people.

ث
02

The artist deserves more than a wall to hang their work on.

Skilled hands go unseen every day — not for lack of talent, but lack of a platform built for them. Kifayat is that platform. We celebrate the maker as much as the made.

ف
03

Community is not an audience. It is participation.

Kifayat is not a gallery you visit and leave. It is a collective you belong to. Workshops, field visits, podcasts, discussions, campus chapters — a living network where everyone grows together.

ج
04

Art must circulate — not just exist.

From canvas and décor to interiors, typography, merchandise, and corporate identity — calligraphy is not a niche. It is a medium that can inhabit every surface, every space that carries meaning.
